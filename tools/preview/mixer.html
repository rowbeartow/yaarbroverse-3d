What changed since you played

  • Everything is much quieter. The sliders start at the levels now in the game, which are the ones you set: master -8, music -21, warnings -15, jumps -26, footsteps -24, menu -12. Normal play (music, footsteps, jumps and landings together) measures -41.6 LUFS at those levels, with the music 4.2 dB under the effects.
  • Everything is softer. The top end above 6,000 hertz is turned down by 9 decibels, every sound now fades in over a few thousandths of a second instead of starting at full, and a soft limiter on the master eases down anything over a third of full and lets nothing past seven tenths.
  • The clanky jump and landing are gone. A landing is now two soft footsteps of the surface (moss or wood) with a low, soft thump under them. A jump is one quiet footstep of the surface as a push-off. There is no separate jump sound.
  • Footsteps and menu clicks sit well under everything else. Warnings, hits and checkpoints are still the clearest, and the music dips under them.
