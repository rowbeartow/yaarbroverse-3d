How a crocodile goes

  • Lurking. It waits low in the water with only its eyes and snout showing. Touching it then does nothing.
  • Swimming after you. Once you are in its stretch of water it comes after you, back awash, at your depth: it follows you down if you dive. It is always slower than you swim.
  • Its tell. Close enough, it fixes its aim on where you are. Its eyes sink under and it creeps at you, pushing a wake ahead of it. On Easy a line also shows where it will lunge; on Normal and Hard there is no line, as with every enemy.
  • The lunge. It shoots along that line, jaws snapping. Only the lunge hurts, at the surface or underwater. Swim out of the line, or dive under it, and it misses: that is the move that gets you past.
  • Resting. After a lunge it lies at the surface getting its breath back: the time to swim on past. Landing on it beats it, and so does Yaarbro’s roll or Caputo’s pounce from a log or the bank.
  • The regen rule. Sent back to a safe bank, you find every crocodile you had not beaten back at home at full strength.

On each difficulty

  • Easy: one in each swim, well to the side. Swim straight across and it never wakes. Its tell lasts 1.8 s, with the line, and it is slow.
  • Normal: one in each swim, right across the way. Its tell lasts 0.9 s, it swims 2.4 m a second (you swim 3.6), and lunges 4.5 m.
  • Hard: two in each swim, faster (2.9), lunging further (5.5 m), a tell of 0.63 s, a shorter rest, and two hits to beat.
  • The bot gets past every one, on every difficulty, as each hero, without losing a heart.
