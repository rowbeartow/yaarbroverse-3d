What to look for

  • The scout on its beat (Normal, Hard). It crosses its bank, stops for a look at each end, and turns back. It is never hidden now, so it is seen from the stumps before the clearing.
  • The chase. When a hero steps onto its bank it stands for half a second, then runs at the hero. From a few metres off it stops, rears up with the acorn and throws. Close in, it does not stop: it runs into the hero, and that costs a heart. Is the half second enough warning for the run?
  • Giving up. When the hero leaves its bank, it turns and goes back to its beat. It never leaves the bank.
  • The tells, with no line. As you asked, the line showing where the acorn or the dive will go is drawn on Easy only. On Normal and Hard the warning is the scout rearing up on its hind legs with the acorn back over its shoulder, and the mosquito tipping nose-down with its legs drawn up, each with its warning sound in the game. Nothing is drawn on the ground or in the air. This page has no sound.
  • The mosquito. On Normal and Hard it stays over a walking hero and dives again soon after each rest. It hovers higher than before, because a hero who jumps a gap in the boughs would otherwise jump up into it.

What is not checked

  • I cannot watch this move. I checked it in still frames and with the rule tests. You are the first to see it at speed.
  • The scout’s run uses its shuffle clip, played fast. It has no run clip of its own. If it looks wrong at speed, a run clip is a model change with its own preview.
  • Hits needed are not changed yet. A scout still takes two hits on every difficulty here. One hit on Easy and Normal, two on Hard, and the rule that a damaged enemy heals when the hero is sent back, are the next pull request.
