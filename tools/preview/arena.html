The chief, its sidekicks, the heroes, the health bar, the speeds and the timing are the game’s own. This page has no sound; in the game the boss music you picked takes over when the fight begins.

How the fight goes

  • It begins when the hero walks in. The bank is a checkpoint on every difficulty. A hero sent back stands on its near edge, outside the fight, and steps in when ready.
  • It throws, then it charges too, then it calls its sidekicks as well. Each third of its health changes its attacks, and it roars at each change. Every attack has a warning first: it rears up before a throw or a call, and lowers itself before a charge.
  • After every attack it rests. That is the opening. A landing on top, Yaarbro’s roll or Caputo’s pounce is a hit. The pips on its bar glow while a hit will count.
  • On Easy any landing on top counts, resting or not, so it can be beaten by jumping on it alone. On Normal and Hard only a hit while it rests counts: at other times a landing bounces off and does no harm.
  • The older player’s rule. Fall off its bank or run out of hearts, and it is back at full health when the hero returns.
  • Beat it and its sidekicks give up, the golden leaf appears on the bough beyond, and nothing can hurt the hero for three seconds.

What to look for

  • Does it read as the chief? It is the scout’s model at 2.3 times the size, with a dark colour laid over its fur. Nothing else is added.
  • The warnings on Normal and Hard, with no lines drawn. Rearing up means acorns or a call. Lowering itself means a charge. On Hard the charge warning is 0.6 seconds: is that fair?
  • The fan of acorns. Three on Normal, five on Hard, spread to either side of where the hero stood. Running sideways clears them.
  • The sidekicks. They fly as the mosquito over the boughs does on that difficulty. On Easy there is one and it is slow. In “Watch” on Easy the test player wins in about ten seconds, before the chief ever calls: to see Easy’s sidekick, play it yourself and take your time.
  • Hard. Eight hits, each needing an opening, with two mosquitoes about from the sixth. The test player takes about 48 seconds. Is that work enough for the older player, or too much with the regen rule?

What is not checked

  • I cannot watch this move. I checked it in still frames and with the rule tests, where the test player beats the chief first time on every difficulty as each hero. You are the first to see it at speed.
  • Its clips are the scout’s. The charge is its shuffle played fast, and the wind-up for a charge is the start of its hop. It has no clips of its own.
  • The test player loses one heart on Normal and none on Easy or Hard. It never runs out.
  • Nobody has heard the fight. Its warnings, throws and hits use the scout’s sounds.
