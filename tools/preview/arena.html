What the clips are

  • Yaarbro: treading water, a front crawl at the surface, a breaststroke underwater, and the climb out. I rigged a copy of his model with Meshy’s Rigging API, the same skeleton his other Meshy files have. Treading (“Swim Idle”), the crawl (“Swim Forward”) and the climb (the end of “Swimming to Edge”) are from Meshy’s animation library. The library has no underwater swim, so that one was made from words with Meshy’s Text to Motion. Each was carried over onto his current model in Blender, bone by bone, because the new rig stands a little differently; his belt and beard cleanup is kept. 27 credits.
  • Caputo: a dog-paddle, an underwater swim and a shake-off, written in his rig script like his other clips. Paddling, his front paws work in turn in circles under his chest, his hind legs kick, his head is held high and his tail streams out. Underwater he goes long and level, front legs sweeping back together, tail waving. When he climbs out he shakes, head first, then shoulders, hips and tail, about seven times a second, and his ears flap. Running cuts the shake short.
  • In the game, each hero treads water when still and swims when moving, swims underwater with the head under, and tips a little along a dive or a swim up.

What to look for

  • Does Yaarbro’s crawl read as swimming at the surface, with his back at the water? Does his treading look like treading?
  • Does Caputo’s paddle read as a dog-paddle, and his underwater swim as swimming? These were written by hand, as his walk and gallop were.
  • The shake-off. Is it worth keeping? It plays after every climb out.
  • The climb out is quick (under half a second): Yaarbro reaches up and over, Caputo uses the push from his jump.

What is not checked

  • I cannot watch this move. I checked it in still frames, side and front, and in the page run forward step by step. You are the first to see it at speed.
