What changed since the first board

  • Recordings replaced the made-in-code sounds, as you asked: jump, landing, mushroom bounce, the hero hit and the golden leaf tune. I went further and replaced every other made sound I could find a recording for: the mushroom footstep, the roll, the pounce, out of hearts, falling, the marmot scout's warning and throw, landing on an enemy, the checkpoint and the menu button.
  • Three sounds are still made in code: the giant mosquito's. The only CC0 insect recordings I could reach are a thin, harsh hiss. A mosquito's whine is close to a plain tone anyway, so it is the made sound most likely to hold up. If it still sounds like a beep to you, say so and I will look further or drop the mosquito's sounds.

What to listen for

  • Whether each recording fits what it is for. I chose them by description and measurement. The ones I am least sure of: the jump (a short swish, about a tenth of a second), the marmot scout's warning (a squeaky toy, lowered), and the mushroom footstep (a step in mud).
  • Anything harsh, sharp or startling. The players are kids. Check the squeak and the mosquito's whine first.
  • Anything that jumps out as louder than the rest. Start the grove music, then go down the page pressing play. Every short sound is brought to one loudness by measurement and then set by its job: warnings, hits and checkpoints on top, what the hero does under them, footsteps under that, music under everything.
  • The golden leaf tune. It is ten seconds long, which is long for "short". A one-second chime and a second fanfare are under Other choices.
  • The mushroom bounce. The pick is a short twang from a real door spring. A longer spring and a recorded synthesizer "boing" are under Other choices.

What you should know

  • I cannot hear any of this. You are the first to hear it. Trust your ears over my descriptions.
  • Every recording is CC0, from OpenGameArt. None is a voice. Nothing came from the 2D game, which has no sound files.
  • The mosquito's hovering whine is an extra, not on your list. It is how the mosquito gets heard before it is on screen. Say no and it goes.
  • Caputo's footsteps are Yaarbro's, made quieter, lighter and softer. There is no separate cat recording.
  • Size: 1.88 MB of sound files, against the 4 MB allowed.
