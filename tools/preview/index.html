The walk, from the side, slowed down

The pale stones are set in the ground, which moves at the speed the game moves him. A paw that is down should stay beside its stone. For smoothness, choose Tenth speed and watch a paw leave the ground and come down again, and watch the moment the slider jumps from the right end back to the left: that is where the loop restarts, and the left hind paw lands there.

The trot, from the side, slowed down

Two beats to a stride. Left hind and right front land together, then right hind and left front. Between beats there is a short moment with no paw down. The back should stay level, the head steady and the tail up.

The run, from the side, slowed down

Four beats to a stride, one paw at a time, going round the body: left hind, right hind, right front, left front. To check, press Pause and drag the slider. The paws land with the slider at about 19, 32, 76 and 89 hundredths of the way along. Between the second and third he is off the ground, stretched long. The left paws are the ones on the far side of him here; Show skeleton makes them easier to follow.

What you asked for, and where it stands

  • PounceNew. Pick “Pounce” under the picture, and turn on Slow motion. He gathers low, drives off his hind legs, goes out long and flat with his front paws reaching ahead and the claws up, and ends with the front paws coming down. It is built from the same parts as his jump, so the two belong together: the same crouch, the same stretch, the tail streaming the same way.
